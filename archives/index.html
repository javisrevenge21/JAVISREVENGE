The archive is the private side of JAVISREVENGE: photos, video, and updates from Ryan Javi's world, often before anyone else hears about them.

It started in 2016 as one place to share everything around Ryan Javi. A lot of it is sent in by people close to the scene or through anonymous tips. Some call it the greatest spam account of all time, for the quality and the sheer volume of posts.

It shows a lot of what can't be posted publicly, but not everything. Only content that is legal to post goes up, and not every post is made by Ryan or is about him. It's a shared space with very few limits, and that's the point.
