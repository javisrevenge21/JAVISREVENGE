Cookie Notice

Effective November 7, 2026

This explains the cookies and browser storage javisrevenge.com uses. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not sell personal information.

Necessary cookies

jr_session keeps you signed in for up to seven days. It is signed, HTTP-only and cannot be read by page scripts. jr_oauth_state, jr_oauth_provider and jr_return_to last ten minutes during sign-in with Google, Microsoft, Discord or GitHub: they protect against request forgery, remember which service you chose and send you back to the page you were on. The site cannot work without these, so they cannot be turned off.

Storage in your browser

These stay on your device and are not sent to us:

  • Music on/off (jr_music_muted) remembers whether you turned the background music off.
  • Song position (jr_music_time, cleared when you close the tab) lets the music continue where it left off as you move between pages.
  • Social embeds choice remembers whether you allowed X and Instagram content on the Socials page.

Third parties

Sign-in pages from Google, Microsoft, Discord and GitHub set their own cookies under their policies. X and Instagram embeds are off by default; if you turn them on, those companies may set cookies. Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music and Tidal are only contacted if you tap one of their links. Our hosting provider, Vercel, may use strictly necessary technical measures for security and performance.

Your controls

You can sign out to remove the session cookie, turn the music off with the music button, and clear cookies and site data in your browser settings at any time. Blocking necessary cookies will stop sign-in from working. Questions: contact@javisrevenge.com.
