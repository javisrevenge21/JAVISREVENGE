JAVISREVENGE

Privacy Notice

Effective November 7, 2026

This notice explains how JAVISREVENGE, the operator of javisrevenge.com, handles personal information. Questions and privacy requests: contact@javisrevenge.com.

Information we collect

Sign-in. When you sign in with Google, Microsoft, Discord or GitHub, we receive your account identifier, name, verified email address and profile picture from that service, and we record which services you have linked. We never receive or store your passwords.

Your account and profile. Your username, display name and anything you add to your profile: bio, location, favorite quote, pronouns, birthday (month and day only), Instagram, X and TikTok handles, interests, mood, title badge, avatar and style choices. We also store when your account was created, recent sign-in times and your email-notification choice.

The Revenge Wall. Posts, replies, photos, videos, PDFs, polls, reactions, poll votes, posts you save, reports you make, who you follow and who follows you, who you block or mute, your photo and video access status and requests, and the notifications we send you (follows, replies, mentions, reaction counts and access decisions).

Membership and payments. If you join REVENGE+, Stripe processes your payment. We receive and store your Stripe customer and subscription IDs, membership status and renewal date. We never see or store your card number.

Profile links. Any Instagram, X, TikTok, Snapchat, Discord, YouTube, Twitch or Threads usernames you add to your profile are shown to members on your profile.

Moderation records. Strike counts, blocked content and the reason it was blocked, reports, hidden posts and bans, including the banned email address so it cannot sign in again.

Agreement record. The date you accepted the Terms and Privacy Notice and your confirmation that you are at least 13.

Contact and tips. Anything you choose to send us, including files.

Technical data. Our hosting and security providers process ordinary data such as IP address, browser and device information, request times and logs.

Who can see what

Other members can see your username, display name, profile and posts only after you have posted on the Revenge Wall. No member can see the list of members, how many members there are, or anyone's email address. Reactions and poll votes are shown only as counts, never names. Follower and following counts appear on profiles, but only you can see the list of who follows you and who you follow; when you follow someone, they are notified. Blocks, mutes, saved posts and your notifications are visible only to you. The site owner can see every account, its email address, linked sign-in services, strikes and moderation history, so the site can be moderated.

How we use it

To sign you in, run your account and profile, show the Revenge Wall to members, send in-site notifications, keep the site safe (including automatic content checks, strikes, reports and bans), respond to requests and, only if you opt in, send you email updates. We do not sell or rent personal information. Sponsored posts on the site are sold directly to sponsors and do not use tracking: we only count how many times each ad was shown and clicked, never who saw or clicked it, and sponsors receive no personal information.

Automatic moderation

Text in posts, replies, usernames and profiles is checked against a short list of words the site owner has banned. Photos and a few still frames from videos are sent to OpenAI's moderation service, which checks only for content sexualizing minors; OpenAI processes them to return a safety result. We do not use facial recognition and do not collect biometric data.

Service providers

Google, Microsoft, Discord and GitHub for sign-in; Vercel for hosting and private storage of accounts, posts and uploads (Vercel Blob); OpenAI for automatic content moderation; Stripe for REVENGE+ payments; Resend for opted-in emails; Google Forms for anonymous tips. Music links open Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music or Tidal only if you tap them. X and Instagram content on the About page loads only after you choose to enable it. Each provider handles data under its own privacy policy.

Retention

Your account, profile and posts are kept while your account is active. Deleting a post removes it and its attachments. Deleting your account from the Account page permanently erases your profile, username, posts, uploads, replies, reactions and poll votes. If an account is removed or banned by the site owner, its profile and posts are deleted; for bans we keep the email address and reason so that it cannot sign in again. Moderation logs are kept for safety and records. We may keep limited records where needed for security, disputes or legal obligations.

Security

We use encrypted connections, signed HTTP-only session cookies, private storage, members-only access to uploads, access controls and data minimization. No online service can promise absolute security.

Your choices and rights

You can edit your profile, delete your posts, turn emails off, unsubscribe from any email, sign out, download a copy of your data from the Account page, or delete your account at any time. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or get a copy of your personal information, and to complain to a privacy regulator. Send requests to the email above; we may need to verify your identity.

Children

You must be at least 13 to join, and everyone confirms their age before getting a username. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 and will delete any such account we find. Contact us if you believe a child has created an account.

Changes

We may update this notice as the site changes. Updates are posted here with a new effective date.
